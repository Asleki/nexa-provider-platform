## Runtime Model Captured

- **Connectivity:** online or offline
- **Environment:** simulation or production
- **Execution authority:** human, AI, assisted, or system
- **Promotion lifecycle:** simulation → human review → production
- Production records never degrade into simulation.
- Simulation promotion preserves source lineage and audit history.
- Simulation payments may use controlled tap/contactless flows, while production payment capabilities can require human PIN entry.
- NexVox AI L3 may generate valid, invalid, edge-case, and recovery scenarios without contaminating production records.
