# 1. Purpose

This document defines the architectural relationship between the Nexa Citizen ID and NexaID.

Although both identifiers are associated with an individual, they belong to different registry families, serve different purposes and are governed by different issuing authorities.

This document establishes the trust model linking public identity with participation in the Nexa Ecosystem.

---

# 2. Scope

This document defines:

- Nexa Citizen ID
- NexaID
- Identity trust
- Collaborative identifier issuance
- KYC readiness
- Identity ownership
- Identity verification
- Identity lifecycle

This document does not define implementation algorithms or cryptographic generation methods.

---

# 3. Architectural Principles

The Master Registry Foundation recognises two independent identity layers.

## Layer 1

Core Infrastructure Identity

Represents the individual within the simulated public infrastructure.

Identifier:

- Nexa Citizen ID

Registry Owner:

- Citizen Registry

---

## Layer 2

Nexa Ecosystem Identity

Represents the same individual within the Nexa Ecosystem.

Identifier:

- NexaID

Registry Owner:

- Identity Registry

---

The two identities are related but remain independently governed.

---

# 4. Nexa Citizen ID

The Nexa Citizen ID serves as the foundational identity for every recognised citizen.

It provides:

- identity uniqueness;
- citizen verification;
- long-term identity continuity;
- trusted identity references.

The Nexa Citizen ID does not provide access to Nexa services by itself.

---

# 5. NexaID

NexaID represents trusted participation within the Nexa Ecosystem.

NexaID enables authorised access to Nexa applications and services including, but not limited to:

- NexaPOS
- NexFarm
- UniFry
- NexaSmart
- NexaPesa
- Future Nexa platforms

NexaID does not replace the Nexa Citizen ID.

---

# 6. Collaborative Identity Issuance

NexaID shall not be generated by a single system.

Its issuance requires controlled collaboration between authorised systems operating under the governance of the Identity Registry.

The collaborative issuance process strengthens trust while preventing independent identity creation by any individual application.

---

# 7. Identity Trust

Identity trust increases as additional verified information becomes available.

Examples include:

- verified names;
- Nexa Citizen ID;
- verified phone number;
- verified business relationships;
- future approved verification sources.

Trust levels determine eligibility for NexaID activation.

The trust evaluation methodology is governed separately from this document.

---

# 8. Registry Ownership

Citizen Registry owns:

- Nexa Citizen ID

Identity Registry owns:

- NexaID

Neither registry owns the other's identifier.

---

# 9. Verification

Authorised systems may verify:

- Nexa Citizen ID;
- NexaID;
- trust status;
- identity relationships.

Verification shall never modify either identifier.

---

# 10. Future Expansion

Future identity capabilities may include:

- biometric verification;
- digital credentials;
- multi-factor trust;
- delegated identity;
- cross-platform identity federation.

Such capabilities shall preserve the independent governance of the Citizen Registry and Identity Registry.
